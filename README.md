## FEATURED PROJECTS

Every repository below has a README covering what it does, its architecture, how it works and how to run it.

<table>
<tr>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### SEPSISSENSE

**Explainable Sepsis Early Warning for the ICU**

Estimates every hour an ICU patient's risk of sepsis in the next six hours and explains why. Trained on all 40,336 PhysioNet 2019 patients on a laptop GPU with a utility-weighted XGBoost + LightGBM ensemble, calibrated, with SHAP explanations. ROC-AUC 0.86 on 8,068 held-out patients; clinician dashboard and patient portal.

`Machine Learning` `XGBoost` `LightGBM` `SHAP` `FastAPI` `React`

[Repository](https://github.com/suriyaprakash-25/SepsisSense)

</td>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### DRIVEPORTZ (DIGITAL TWIN)

**Car Ownership & Garage Marketplace**

Gives every car a "vehicle passport": service history, health score, resale estimate and ownership transfer. Owners book garages and pay online; garages and admins handle GST invoices, earnings, settlements and disputes.

`Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB` `React` `Cashfree` `CI`

[Repository](https://github.com/suriyaprakash-25/Digital-Twin) · [Live Project](https://www.driveportz.com/)

</td>

</tr>
<tr>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### AGENTSHELF

**Storefront for AI Shopping Agents · PayPal AI Hackathon**

Small sellers turn WhatsApp photos into listings in an MCP storefront that AI shopping agents can search and quote from. Checkout uses PayPal Orders v2 with a human approving every payment; webhooks, tracking, refunds and invoices run through a job worker.

`MCP` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `PayPal` `React` `Docker`

[Repository](https://github.com/suriyaprakash-25/AgentShelf) · [Live Demo](https://agentshelf-frontend.vercel.app)

</td>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### MEDFLOW GUARDIAN

**Patient-Controlled Medical Records**

Hospitals can read a record only within the patient's current consent, enforced centrally by the backend. Time-boxed cross-hospital access, malware scanning of uploads, FHIR R4 import/export, MFA/OIDC login and full audit logs, with separate patient, doctor and admin portals.

`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `FHIR` `OIDC` `React` `Render`

[Repository](https://github.com/suriyaprakash-25/medflow-guardian)

</td>

</tr>
<tr>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### REDROB RANKER

**Candidate Ranking Pipeline · Redrob Hackathon**

Ranks the top 100 of 100,000 candidates for a job description: fake-profile detection, structured job-fit scoring, TF-IDF/SVD semantic matching, claimed-vs-tested skill checks and fact-based reasoning. No LLM calls; about a minute on a CPU.

`Python` `scikit-learn` `TF-IDF` `Ranking` `Hugging Face`

[Repository](https://github.com/suriyaprakash-25/redrob-ranker) · [Demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Suriya-25/redrob-ranker)

</td>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### BORESAKSHI

**Borewell Decision Support for Farmers · MSME Idea Hackathon 6.0**

Helps Tamil Nadu farmers decide where to drill. Rig operators log real drilling results, admins verify them, and a public accuracy ledger scores earlier predictions against those results, alongside an India-wide groundwater outlook.

`React` `Leaflet` `Express` `MongoDB` `FastAPI` `ML`

[Repository](https://github.com/suriyaprakash-25/BoreSakshi)

</td>

</tr>
<tr>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### COUNTERGUARD AI

**Multi-Agent Counterfeit Listing Investigator**

A LangGraph team of agents investigates marketplace listings for counterfeit or grey-market risk and produces a 0–100 risk score with evidence and optional LLM reasoning. React dashboard, Chrome extension and graph/vector storage.

`LangGraph` `FastAPI` `Neo4j` `ChromaDB` `React` `Chrome Extension`

[Repository](https://github.com/suriyaprakash-25/counterguard-ai)

</td>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### AUTOSHIELD EDGE AI

**In-Vehicle CAN Bus Intrusion Detection**

A virtual proof of concept for spotting attacks on a car's internal network: rules (ID allow-list, rate limits, message length) combined with frozen machine-learning models, demonstrated on six attack scenarios in a live React/D3 dashboard.

`Python` `scikit-learn` `Cybersecurity` `React` `D3`

[Repository](https://github.com/suriyaprakash-25/AutoShield-Edge-AI)

</td>

[Repository](https://github.com/suriyaprakash-25/Trackit) · [Live (earlier version)](https://inv-gdg.vercel.app/home)
